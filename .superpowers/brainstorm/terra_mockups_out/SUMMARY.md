# Structural Verdict

The proposed Mission / Recipe / Evidence three-column layout names the right ingredients, but I would not ship it as the cockpit's primary structure. Mission selection is a brief, discrete decision at the beginning of a trial. Recipe editing is continuous work, and the prompt needs real width to remain legible alongside its reference strip. Giving Mission a permanent column makes the composition feel balanced in a diagram but spends a third of the desktop on a control that should recede after selection.

The stronger shape is a mission strip above a two-pane workbench. The left pane combines the active trial brief and the recipe, so the selected purpose stays visible without competing with prompt editing. The right pane remains a dedicated evidence inspector because nearby prompts, coverage context, and candidate history are useful while the recipe changes. This preserves the spec's important honesty: evidence is context from already-computed work, never a fake score forecast. The bottom drawer remains right for queued trials and grouped results because neither needs to displace active drafting.

This is not a call to reduce the page to a generic form. The mission strip still makes the four generation intents first-class, while the two working panes reflect the real loop: formulate a trial, consult what exists nearby, and explicitly spend GPU time. On a narrower screen the panes stack naturally, whereas three desktop columns would collapse into a more brittle hierarchy.

`mockup-a-two-pane-trial-workbench.html` demonstrates the recommended mission-strip plus Recipe/Evidence workbench and Queue-and-Results drawer.

`mockup-a-v2-claws-and-collage.html` keeps that structure but gives mission choices torn, uneven paper silhouettes and uses a taped, rotated thumbnail treatment for every nearby image, directly echoing the torn supports, seams, and tape edges in the CLAWMARKS source work. Recipe and Evidence labels use ultramarine and crimson redaction bars, while the quiet, seam-led editing surface preserves the source work's blacked-out-bar motif without turning the research tool into decoration.

`mockup-a-v3-target-picker-tooltips-theme.html` adds a three-card coverage-cell picker that updates both the recipe target and evidence crop, keeping the decision compact and grounded in nearby work. It adds short plain-language hover and focus tooltips for the few ML terms that need them. The light scheme uses unbleached paper, kraft-card fields, warm charcoal ink, and adjusted accent contrast rather than a neutral gray inversion.

`mockup-a-v4-mobile-hierarchy.html` collapses a chosen mission into a compact current-mission row, then restores the paper-scrap picker only when the user chooses change. On narrow screens it gives the prompt and generate control a stronger, earlier visual block while moving secondary configuration below it. Evidence opens as a compact contextual summary and expands only on request, so it supports rather than competes with drafting.

`mockup-a-v5-critique-fixes-autopilot.html` makes every help affordance tap-toggleable and distinguishes coverage-cell choices from editable fields with card-paper surfaces, acid rails, and selected checks. It keeps the brief, controls, and advanced settings in compact multi-column groups longer, separates the estimate from its trial-record note, and synchronizes the queue preview to live recipe controls. The cream theme now has stronger work-surface and tape separation, while the stamp stack includes common condensed fallbacks. Its third drawer tab, Autopilot, offers evidence-cited, editable draft suggestions that only load into the Recipe and Queue view, never forecast a score or run a trial.
